Santoryu - Three Sword Style

Roronoa Zoro is the swordsman of the Straw Hat Pirates and one of the most powerful characters in the *One Piece* universe. Trained by Dracule Mihawk, the world's strongest swordsman, Zoro wields three swords with deadly precision. His dream is to become the greatest swordsman in the world. Known for his unmatched willpower, loyalty to Luffy, and the sheer intensity of his techniques like *Oni Giri* and *Asura*, Zoro slices through all odds on the Grand Line.
