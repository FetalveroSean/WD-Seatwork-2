The Bond of Arthur

Sylvie Leywin is the daughter of Sylvia Indrath and Agrona Vritra, as well as the granddaughter of Kezess Indrath and Myre. She is the bond of Arthur Leywin, who was entrusted to him by her mother Sylvia. Sylvie hatches in the Kingdom of Elenoir and she and Arthur go on many adventures together. She accompanies him to Xyrus Academy and later to Epheotus, where she trains with her maternal grandparents.

Sylvie is not just a companion she's a descendant of dragons and one of the strongest beings alive.
She undergoes transformation and evolves symbolizing her growth alongside Arthur.
Sylvie shares a telepathic bond with Arthur able to sense emotions and even speak mentally.
