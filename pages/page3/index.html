Short Info

Elijah Knight is a major supporting character in The Beginning After The End. An orphan raised by the Elder Dwarf Rahdeas, he was known for his metal magic affinity and served as a representative from the Kingdom of Darv. Close friends with Arthur Leywin, he became a student at Xyrus Academy. During a traumatic moment, Elijah lost control of his body, overtaken by an unknown force — a mysterious event that led to his abduction during an attack and presumed transfer to Alacrya. His quiet intellect, loyalty, and tragic arc made him one of the most complex characters in the story.
