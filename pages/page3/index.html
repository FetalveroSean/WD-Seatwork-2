Elijah Knight

Elijah Knight is a major supporting character of The Beginning After The End. He is an orphan raised by the Elder Dwarf Rahdeas and a former Adventurer who acted as a representative from the Kingdom of Darv. While he was seemingly of human origin, Elijah was shown to have an affinity for metal magic. During his time as an Adventurer, he was acquainted with and became close friends with Arthur Leywin, where thanks to Arthur's influence, Elijah would become a student of Xyrus Academy under the sponsorship of Vincent Helstea. During the attack on Xyrus Academy, after seeing Arthur's childhood friend Tessia Eralith in the hands of the revenge-seeking Lucas Wykes, he seemingly passes out, letting an unknown force control his body. In Elijahs' words, "as if some inner force pushed my consciousness out, my body felt like it wasn't mine anymore... like I was an entirely different person simply spectating my body from behind". During this episode, after his body reached its' limit, he was kidnapped by the attackers and presumed to be taken back to Alacrya.
